The seven upstream WOFF2 subsets were decoded and combined into one OpenType font with fontTools 4.60.1 for native CoreText loading. Glyph outlines and family names are unchanged.

Nunito and Comic Shanns are also from the same official revision under SIL OFL 1.1; their original font descriptors and license notices are included. Upstream WOFF2 subsets were combined in the same way.
